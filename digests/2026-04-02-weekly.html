War's End Could Reshape Markets This Week

What moved
  • US stocks rebounded 70bps as Iran war risks fade; equities still down 4.1% YTD as growth slows to 2.2%, half pre-pandemic trend.
  • Japan equities cratered 250bps to +1.2% YTD after industrial output fell 2.1% despite bullish guidance; growth at 0.8% signals the Bank of Japan hiked too soon.
  • Brazil stocks surged 17.1% YTD despite a 12% policy rate and fiscal deficit blowing to 6.7% of GDP; currency weakness (USD/BRL at 5.17) is pricing in depreciation risk.
  • Oil rallied 8.3% YTD to $107 WTI; Middle East tensions and Russian sanctions keep crude elevated, feeding inflation into transport and goods costs.
What to watch
  • US April jobs report (due next week): any acceleration in jobless claims past 250k would trigger Fed pivot talk and validate recession scenarios.
  • China Caixin PMI weakness (50.8) versus official 4.7% GDP growth signals small exporters are suffering; watch April trade data for tariff impact evidence.
  • India RBI rate decision (May 29): if Q4 GDP misses 6%, expect 50bps cut; rupee weakness and current account deficit widening (to 1.2%) constrain easing room.
  • UK labor data (May/June): unemployment at 5% and rising signals National Insurance hikes are working as predicted; further rises validate recession forecasts.
  • ECB inflation surprise (May CPI): recent jump to 2.2% in eurozone has stalled rate cuts; softer reading unlocks 2-3 cuts toward 1.5% by year-end.
