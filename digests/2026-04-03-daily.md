# Oil Shock Masks Labour Market Healing

- WTI crude spiked 4.7% to $112, forcing central banks to choose between growth support and inflation credibility.
- US Fed repriced rate cuts from 47% to 31% odds in one week; recession probability halved to 30% on stimulus bets, not fundamentals.
- Japan's capex growth collapsed from 12.2% to 9.7% despite 2.5% unemployment, a mismatch that precedes layoffs within six months.
- Brazil held rates at 12% into 1.7% growth; fiscal dominance now runs monetary policy, not inflation.
