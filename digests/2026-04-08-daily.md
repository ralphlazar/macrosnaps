# Iran Ceasefire Crushes Oil, Stocks Soar on Peace Bet

- US-Iran ceasefire sent WTI crude down 16.2% and Brent down 14.9% in single session, the biggest oil move in months.
- Japan equities jumped 5.5 percentage points YTD to 8.6% as geopolitical risk premiums collapsed across global markets.
- Germany and France stocks rallied hard but remain deeply underwater YTD, exposing how much structural weakness persists beneath the peace rally.
- Silver surged 6.4% and gold added 3.4% as safe-haven demand flipped to risk-on, signaling genuine sentiment shift not just commodities rebalancing.
