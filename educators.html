Use MacroSnaps
in your teaching

☀️ ☁️ ⛈️

Free, no signup, works on any device. Every metric is explained at three levels, ideal for introductory lectures, seminar discussions, or self-directed student research across 12 major economies.

Three levels, one screen
Beginner
Plain-English explanations. Numbers in context. No jargon. Good for first-year undergrads or anyone new to macro.
Moderate
Cause, effect, and policy linkages. Assumes basic economic literacy. Good for second-year and above.
Expert
Market mechanics, monetary transmission, and macro interdependencies. Good for postgrads and professionals.
Teaching ideas
☀️ Open a country card mid-lecture to show live CPI, unemployment, or policy rate data. Toggle the expertise level to match your class.
☁️ Use the weather icons as a quick visual prompt (sunny, cloudy, or stormy) to spark discussion about which economies are thriving and which are under pressure, before diving into the data.
⛈️ Set a discussion question: ask students to explain a metric at beginner level, then read the expert version together to see what they missed.
☀️ Start each class with that day's story bullets. Concise, three-act daily narratives covering the most important macro event, biggest market movers, and how they connect. A ready-made warm-up in under two minutes.
