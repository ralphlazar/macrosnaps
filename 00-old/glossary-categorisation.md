# Glossary Categorisation - 174 entries

## Categories
- **macro** - GDP, inflation, employment, fiscal policy, monetary policy, central bank tools
- **credit** - bonds, spreads, ratings, default, debt instruments
- **equity** - stock markets, valuations, volatility, indices
- **fx** - currencies, exchange rates, carry trade, regimes
- **trade** - balance of payments, current account, tariffs, capital flows
- **institutions** - central banks, international organisations, regulatory bodies
